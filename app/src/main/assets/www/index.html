Robot Yard

Voltnutt and the crew are loose in the toy factory yard.

  • 👆Drag to look around. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • 🤖Tap a robot to poke it, chat, or drive it.
  • ✋Press and hold a robot, Rust Mite or toy to pick it up. Drag it around, then let go to drop it or flick to throw.
  • 🏭Tap Stage to visit any of the 8 toy factories.
  • 📦Pick a robot or toy at the bottom, then tap the yard to drop it in.
  • ⚔️Each robot has its card-game power and ultimate. Drop Rust Mites and a Target Core from Training to practice.
  • 🔋Robots get sleepy when their battery runs low. Drop a battery or let them find the charging bay.

This sandbox needs 3D graphics (WebGL), and this browser has it switched off. Try opening it in Chrome.
